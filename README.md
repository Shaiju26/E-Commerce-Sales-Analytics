# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics | End-to-End Data Analytics Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a complete **end-to-end data analytics workflow** using an e-commerce sales dataset.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**.

The project covers data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL-based business analysis, dashboard development, and recommendation generation to support data-driven decision-making.

---

# 🏢 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data every day. However, raw data alone does not provide clear visibility into sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and business growth opportunities.

This project answers key business questions:

- Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which categories contribute most to business growth?
- Which regions and cities perform best?
- Which promotions generate maximum sales?
- How do discounts impact profitability?
- Which products require pricing or inventory optimization?

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare raw sales data for analysis.
- Perform exploratory data analysis using Python.
- Analyze business performance using SQL.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Identify key revenue and profitability drivers.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

# 💡 Business Insights

## Product Insights

- Electronics contributed approximately **74% of total revenue and profit**.
- High-performing products included:
  - iPhone 14
  - MacBook Air
  - Sony Bravia TV
  - Samsung Galaxy S21
  - HP Pavilion

These products should receive priority for inventory planning and marketing investment.

---

## Promotion Insights

- **Summer Sale (Email)** and **Weekend Flash Sale (Mobile Push)** generated the strongest sales performance.
- Lower-performing campaigns require optimization or redesign.

---

## Discount Insights

- Profit margin remained approximately **10% across discount levels**.
- Higher discounts did not significantly improve profitability.
- Discount strategies should focus on increasing sales volume while protecting margins.

---

## Regional Insights

High-performing regions included:

- Madhya Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Uttar Pradesh

Top-performing cities included:

- Bhopal
- Indore
- Kanpur
- Lucknow

---

# 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory availability for high-performing Electronics products.
- Optimize discount strategies to improve profitability.
- Allocate marketing budgets toward successful campaigns.
- Review underperforming promotional activities.
- Expand business operations in high-performing regions.
- Monitor product profitability regularly.
